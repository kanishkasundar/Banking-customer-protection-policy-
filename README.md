# Banking-customer-protection-policy-

A corporate policy review of Indian Overseas Bank’s Customer Protection Policy, focusing on customer rights, responsibilities, fraud protection, compensation, and grievance redressal.
The case study evaluates IOB’s compliance, governance mechanisms, policy effectiveness, business relevance, and areas for improvement.
It also provides practical recommendations for strengthening customer protection, risk management, and regulatory compliance.
